# Quran Hikmah data: language packs

Optional downloads for the free app **Quran Majeed - Baitul Hikmah** (no ads, no in-app purchases, no sign-in).
Each pack holds translations of the meanings of the Quran and Al-Mukhtasar tafsir from
[QuranEnc.com](https://quranenc.com), **unmodified**: every text is exactly as QuranEnc publishes it,
built from its original data: its SQLite downloads (`packs-2026.11.10`), or the raw responses of its
live API, which carries the latest text (everything since, and the Russian Mukhtasar).
No word-by-word is included (licence unclear).

Manifest: `https://github.com/mohkh77/quran-hikmah-data/releases/latest/download/manifest.json`

## QuranEnc.com terms

> Contents of the translations can be downloaded and re-published, with the following terms and conditions:
> 1. No modification, addition, or deletion of the content.
> 2. Clearly referring to the publisher and the source (QuranEnc.com).
> 3. Mentioning the version number when re-publishing the translation.
> 4. Keeping the transcript information inside the document.
> 5. Notifying the source (QuranEnc.com) of any note on the translation.
> 6. Updating the translation according to the latest version issued from the source (QuranEnc.com).
> 7. Inappropriate advertisements must not be included when displaying translations of the meanings of the Noble Quran.

Where QuranEnc publishes no version number, a text is credited with its date: "QuranEnc.com, file dated
<date>" (the date inside its download) or "QuranEnc.com API, fetched <date>", until QuranEnc gives one.
QuranEnc names no translator for the Bulgarian, Georgian and Iranun translations (credited to
QuranEnc.com), and titles the Shona and Zulu ones and the Urdu As-Sa'di only in Arabic (given in English,
with the Arabic title in brackets).

## More translations for English, Urdu and Pashto

The app includes English (Saheeh International), Urdu (Jalandhari) and Pashto (Rowwad). The "more
translations" packs add QuranEnc's other translations of those languages, and the Urdu As-Sa'di tafsir,
which QuranEnc publishes by passage: each passage's parts are kept exactly as published, in its order.

## Fonts

The Malayalam and Yoruba packs include a font so that their text displays correctly on every phone
(Noto Sans Malayalam and Noto Sans, from the Noto project). Each is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1,
whose text is in the pack (`fonts/OFL.txt`).
